Privacy Policy

Effective 4 October 2026

The short version. Your chats stay on your iPhone. When you ask Boo for a read, the screenshots and messages you added go through our server to Google's Gemini AI, which writes the answer. We don't store them, we don't sell anything, there are no ads, no tracking and no account with your name or email.

Who we are

Cooked is made by David Kosor, an independent developer in Norway, who is the data controller for the processing described here. Contact: kosordavid@gmail.com.

What stays on your iPhone

These are saved only on your device and never leave it unless you ask Boo for a read (see below):

  • Your conversations: the screenshots and messages you add, Boo's reads and suggested replies.
  • What you tell Boo about yourself: first name, age, city, who you're texting, your goal and texting style.
  • What Boo remembers about each person: facts they shared, inside jokes and plans.
  • What Boo learns about you: which reply styles you pick and your last few copied or edited replies, so new replies sound like you. You can reset this in Settings > Reset what Boo learned.
  • Your settings, such as Boo's voice and language.

You can delete all conversations in Settings > Delete all conversations. Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device.

What we send when you ask Boo

When you tap to cook a read, ask Boo a question or ask for new replies, the app sends over an encrypted connection:

  • The screenshots and messages in that conversation, and anything you typed to Boo.
  • The details you gave about yourself (as listed above), what Boo remembers about that person, the reply style you chose and a short summary of the styles and recent replies you picked, so the replies sound like you.

This goes to the Cooked server on Google Cloud Run (Belgium region), which passes it to Google's Gemini model on Vertex AI to produce the answer and returns the answer to your phone. Our server does not save the content of your requests and does not write it to logs. Under Google Cloud's terms, Google processes this data as our processor, does not use it to train its models, and may hold it briefly in memory while handling the request.

Screenshots can include information about other people, such as their name, photos and messages. Only add content you're comfortable having processed this way. Cooked uses it only to give you advice and never shows it to anyone else.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. We never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple on your device whether your subscription is active.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of your data for marketing.
  • No reading of your photo library: Cooked only sees the screenshots you pick.

Legal basis (GDPR)

We process the content you send to Boo to provide the service you ask for (contract, Article 6(1)(b)). Usage counters and abuse checks rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and affordable (Article 6(1)(f)).

Service providers and transfers

Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Vertex AI and Firestore) and Firebase, both operated by Google, process data for us under Google's data processing terms. Our server runs in the EU. Gemini requests may be processed by Google outside the EU, under the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of personal data, object to processing, or ask for a copy. Most data exists only on your phone, where you control it. Because the server only keeps an anonymous ID and short-lived counters, we can usually not link a request to you, but write to us and we'll help. You can also complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) or your local authority.

Age

Cooked is for adults 18 and over. We don't knowingly process data from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
