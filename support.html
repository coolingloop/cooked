Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email kosordavid@gmail.com and you'll get a reply within two working days.

How do I use Cooked?

Screenshot the chat (or their Tinder, Hinge or Instagram profile), tap Add and pick the screenshots. Boo gives you a verdict from Ice cold to Cooked to perfection, a read on the person, when to reply and three replies to send. Tap a reply to copy it, or the pencil to make it yours.

What do the colours on the replies mean?

Each reply has a style: Chill (blue), Playful (orange), Flirty (pink), Bold (red) and Spicy (deep pink). Boo picks the mix by matching the other person's energy. Tap the style button above the replies to get only one style, or tell Boo what to focus on.

Does Boo learn?

Yes. Boo remembers facts, plans and inside jokes for each person, and learns which styles and wording you actually send. It's all kept on your iPhone. Reset it any time in Settings.

Boo read a message wrong

Tap Chat to see the messages Boo read, fix any that are wrong, then cook again. Clear, uncropped screenshots work best.

How do I cancel Cooked Pro?

On your iPhone, open Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Cooked and tap Cancel. Apple handles all billing, so we can't cancel or refund for you; you can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro but it isn't active

Open Cooked's paywall and tap Restore, signed in with the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I delete my data?

Your conversations live on your iPhone: use Settings > Delete all conversations, or delete the app. Our server keeps only an anonymous ID and usage counters that delete themselves within about three days. See the Privacy Policy.
